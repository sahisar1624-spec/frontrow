Hair

Cut, coloured and finished around how your hair actually falls.

Hair is where Front Row starts. Every cut and colour is built from a real consultation, not a template pulled off a mood board — precision cutting, full colour and colour correction, balayage, keratin-smoothing treatments and the wash & blow-dry that's become a signature booking on its own. We work with foil-gold heritage names like L’Oréal, Inoa and Schwarzkopf Professional for colour that holds its depth in daylight, Olaplex and K18 to protect and rebuild the hair's bond while we're in there, and Nashi Argan for a finish that still looks intentional three days after you leave the chair. Whether it's a first cut or a full transformation, every hair appointment gets the same unhurried, precise attention.

Nails

Never squeezed into a lunch break.

Our nail bar is set up for the two-hour mani-pedi as comfortably as a fifteen-minute polish change — classic and gel manicures, the Classic Pedicure our regulars keep rebooking, sculpted extensions built to your natural nail bed, and hand-painted nail art for the days that call for a little more front row. We finish with Essie and Klio for colour that's still glossy weeks later, and reach for Lycon and Rica where a service calls for wax rather than polish. Solo appointment or a mani-pedi booked with a friend in the next chair — the standard doesn't change: real technique, real time, a finish worth choosing again.

Body & Face

Time set aside on purpose — not squeezed between two other bookings.

Beyond hair and nails, Front Row is a full beauty stop — waxing, threading and bleaching for face and body, facials tuned to what your skin needs that week rather than a fixed routine, and massage with real, unhurried time behind it. Our treatment rooms use Dermalogica and Esthemax for facials that genuinely address the skin in front of us, and Lycon and Rica for waxing gentle enough for sensitive areas and thorough enough to last. The Full Arms, Full Legs & Underarms package — three services, one visit — is one of our most-booked combinations for exactly that reason. Every body and face appointment ends the way every Front Row appointment should: unhurried, and worth the trip back.
