Terms & Conditions

The fine print, in full.

1. Registration fee

A non-refundable deposit of AED 500 (+ VAT 5%) is charged upon commencement of membership.

2. Expiry

Membership expires at the determined date shown on the card, regardless of the extent to which the benefit or available membership services and facilities have been used.

3. Sharing & transfer

Membership can be transferred to, or shared with, one (1) additional member — family or friend.

4. Discount

20% discount applies on all salon services year-round, excluding promotions.

5. Pricing changes

Prices are subject to change without prior notice.

6. Late arrivals

Management is entitled to regretfully cancel any reservation in the case of delays that exceed 15 minutes, as delays do impact existing reservations made by other members.

7. Redemption after expiry

Any remaining Annual Membership amount (AED 500 + VAT 5%) can be redeemed for salon services after the date of expiry, within 3 months.
